About Me

I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Engineering at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, with research interests in NLP, speech technologies and machine learning for low-resource African languages. I supervise MSc and PhD research and collaborate on interdisciplinary AI projects that serve local communities.

Core strengths: curriculum design, research supervision, model adaptation for low-data settings, and community-focused dataset curation.
